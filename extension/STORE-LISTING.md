# Chrome Web Store listing copy

## Short description

Review the displayed price without inventing mandatory fees or selecting optional extras. Calculations stay in your browser.

## Detailed description

PriceTruth adds clear price context on supported hotel, flight, and ticket
sites. Open the on-page badge for an integer-cents breakdown of the displayed
amount. Current U.S. hotel and live-event ticket prices are treated as
mandatory-fee inclusive, and optional extras are never selected for you.
Taxes or other checkout costs that the page does not attest stay visibly
unknown, so the extension labels the displayed amount as a known subtotal
instead of promising a complete checkout total.
The overlay stays silent unless it sees both an explicitly USD-denominated
price and reliable U.S. offer or point-of-sale evidence.

Every projection and unknown cost is labeled. PriceTruth never presents a modeled amount as a
seller quote, and it stays silent when it cannot identify a price confidently.
If detection is wrong, correct the amount locally in one step.

The bundled calculator also reviews hotels, flights, event tickets,
subscriptions, and retail purchases without leaving the popup. Projected
subscription costs remain explicitly labeled.

Privacy is part of the architecture: calculations happen locally; there are no
analytics or automatic network requests; and only per-site settings are stored.

## Store fields the operator must supply

- Public support email and support URL
- Deployed privacy-policy URL
- 1280×800 or 640×400 screenshots captured from supported pages with test data
- 440×280 promotional tile if the store requests one
- Single-purpose and permission-use declarations copied from `PRIVACY.md`
- Territory, language, pricing, and trader-status declarations

Do not claim seller partnerships, guaranteed checkout totals, or support beyond
the adapters listed in `README.md`.
