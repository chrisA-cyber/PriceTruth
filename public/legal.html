Privacy policy

Applies to the PriceTruth website, account dashboard, API, and browser-extension preview.

The short version: PriceTruth does not sell personal information or use advertising trackers. An account stores only what is needed to sign you in, run saved reports and alerts, provide a purchased plan, secure API access, and honor your preferences. You can export or delete account-owned data from the dashboard.

Information you provide

  • Account: email address, verification state, plan, creation time, notification preferences, and time zone.
  • Saved activity: products you save, alert thresholds and status, and the searches or manual inputs needed to produce a report.
  • Developer access: API-key label, tier, prefix, usage, and timestamps. Secret keys are shown once and stored only as cryptographic hashes.
  • Billing: plan and transaction references. Card details go directly to the payment processor and are not stored by PriceTruth.

Essential browser storage

Signing in sets an HTTP-only pt_session cookie. It is same-site, secure on HTTPS deployments, inaccessible to page scripts, and used only for authentication. A separate anti-forgery token protects account changes. The browser may also store your local comparison list and dismissal of the privacy notice; these stay on that device. PriceTruth does not set advertising cookies.

Security and service records

The service may process short-lived rate-limit data and cryptographic hashes of an IP address or browser identifier to protect sessions and prevent abuse. Operational logs should contain request method, route, status, duration, and error category—not API secrets, sign-in tokens, full query strings, card data, or alert-email contents.

Processors and data sources

Depending on deployment configuration, PriceTruth may use a hosting/database provider, a transactional-email provider, Stripe for payment and subscription management, and category-specific pricing providers. Each receives only what its task requires. A seller or pricing provider may receive the search phrase needed to retrieve a listing. Outbound seller sites follow their own policies.

Why information is processed

Account and service data is used to perform the service you request, secure it, fulfill a contract for paid access, comply with law, and—in the case of optional alerts or digests—act on your consent. Essential account and billing messages are not marketing.

Retention and deletion

  • Single-use sign-in links expire after 15 minutes and are invalid after use.
  • Sessions normally expire after 30 days and may be configured for no more than 90 days.
  • Saved reports, alerts, preferences, and active keys remain while the account is active or until you remove them.
  • Deleting an account removes account-owned watches, alerts, preferences, notification subscriptions, and sign-in tokens; revokes sessions and keys; and de-identifies the account and billing ledger where a minimal transaction record must remain for integrity, tax, fraud, or legal obligations. An active paid subscription must first be canceled and reflected in account status.

Your choices and rights

The dashboard lets you access saved data, download a portable JSON export, change notification preferences, unsubscribe, revoke keys, and delete your account. These controls support access, correction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent, and erasure rights where applicable. PriceTruth does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is no sale to opt out of.

Children and international use

PriceTruth is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum digital-consent age in their region. Do not submit a child’s personal information. Service data may be processed in the United States; a launch into a region requiring additional transfer terms or a local representative must publish those details before targeted availability there.

Questions or security reports

Use the self-service dashboard for account data. This deployment has no published private support channel and therefore must not accept payment. Never post personal information, credentials, or vulnerability details in a public repository issue.

Terms of service

What the service does

PriceTruth combines an advertised amount with seller-listed, catalog, market-typical, or modeled costs to estimate an all-in price. It may also store observations, summarize history, compare reports, and notify you about a target.

PriceTruth reports are not seller quotes. Prices, taxes, inventory, rules, and fees can change or depend on details the report does not know. The seller’s final checkout is authoritative. Verify the same item, dates, travelers, quantities, options, location, cancellation terms, and recurring terms immediately before paying.

Price-data methodology

Every report line uses one of these evidence classes:

  • Seller-listed: supplied by you or returned by a seller/data source. It is not an independent guarantee.
  • Catalog or market-typical: based on dated plan data, category norms, or a representative market value—not a live quote.
  • Modeled: computed from assumptions or heuristics and may differ materially from checkout.

Confidence summarizes evidence completeness; it is not a probability that the final price is exact. Deal quality combines recorded all-in history and fee load. A score is informational, never personalized financial advice, and never changes because a seller pays or participates in an affiliate program. Illustrative seed history is labeled and must not be represented as observed market history.

Use “Edit assumptions” to replace modeled inputs. When a direct source fails, the report must say it has degraded and identify the fallback model. Corrections should preserve provenance rather than silently relabeling estimates as listed prices.

Affiliate disclosure

Compensated and affiliate links are disabled unless a specific material-connection disclosure appears beside the action before you leave PriceTruth.

Affiliate status is never an input to a true-price amount, evidence label, confidence value, comparison, or deal score. A partner cannot pay to hide a fee, lower an all-in price, improve a score, or outrank a better result. Compensated links are labeled near the action and use appropriate sponsored-link attributes. If a report has no relevant seller URL, PriceTruth shows a verification checklist instead of sending you to an unrelated partner.
