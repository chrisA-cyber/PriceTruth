Privacy Policy

PriceTruth (prototype), operated by [COMPANY ENTITY] · contact [CONTACT EMAIL]

The short version: no accounts, no cookies, no trackers, no analytics, no third-party requests. The only personal data this software stores is an email address, and only if you choose to create a price alert. We never sell or share personal data. Ask us to delete your alert email and we will.

What we collect, and when

Price alerts — the only personal data. If you create a price alert we store the email address you enter, the product you chose, and your price threshold. That is the entire record, and it is collected only when you submit the alert form. Browsing, analyzing prices, and viewing history collect nothing. In the current prototype, alerts are stored but no email is ever sent — no mail provider is connected. A production version would send alerts only after double opt-in confirmation.

B2B API keys. Commercial API users get a key; we store a label they choose, the tier, and a daily usage count. The key itself is stored only as a SHA-256 hash — we cannot recover it and it never appears in logs.

What we do not collect:

  • No accounts, usernames, passwords, or profiles.
  • No cookies of any kind — the server never sets one.
  • No analytics, pixels, fingerprinting, or session recording.
  • No third-party requests: everything is served from our own origin and our Content-Security-Policy blocks the rest. Our server makes no outbound network calls.
  • No IP logging. IPs are used transiently, in memory only, for rate limiting, and are discarded within minutes — never written to disk.
  • No query strings or search terms in logs — only the HTTP method, path, status code, and response time.
  • A no-referrer policy, so outbound clicks do not tell the destination site what you were looking at here.

Why, and on what basis

The alert email exists for exactly one purpose: sending you the alert you asked for. No marketing, no enrichment, no combining with other data (there is no other data). Where GDPR-style analysis applies, the lawful basis is consent: you typed your email into the alert form. You can withdraw it any time by asking us to delete the alert.

Where it lives

All data sits in a local SQLite database file on the machine running the prototype. No cloud databases, no third-party processors, no international transfers.

No sale, no sharing

We do not sell personal information and do not share it with third parties — not with advertisers, data brokers, or affiliate partners. Affiliate links carry a partner tag, never your personal data.

Retention and deletion

Alert records are kept while the alert is active; the prototype has no automated retention schedule yet, and a production deployment would define and publish one. Deletion on request: email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address on the alert and we delete every record tied to it.

Your rights — GDPR

This prototype is not marketed to the EU public, but we honor GDPR principles by design: access (ask, and we will tell you exactly what we hold — at most your address, alert product, and threshold) and erasure (ask, and we delete it). There is no profiling, no automated decision-making with legal effect, and no special-category data. A production EU-facing deployment would name a controller and processors and provide a supervisory-authority complaint route.

Your rights — CCPA/CPRA

The prototype does not meet CCPA business thresholds, but our practices already exceed its requirements: we do not sell personal information and do not “share” it for cross-context behavioral advertising — there is nothing to opt out of. Right to know and right to delete are honored via [CONTACT EMAIL], and we never discriminate for exercising them.

Children's privacy

PriceTruth is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region) and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child submitted an email address, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If the prototype grows into a product this policy will change — a production version adds a mail provider, double opt-in, and a real retention schedule. Changes are posted here with a new effective date; material changes to how alert emails are used would require fresh consent.

Affiliate Disclosure

Per the FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255): if a link can earn us money, you get told before you rely on it.

If you click through to a partner site from PriceTruth and then book or buy, PriceTruth may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. That is a “material connection” under FTC rules, and this page — plus the interstitial you always see before leaving — is how we disclose it.

How our outbound links work

  • Every partner link goes through our own /go route, never directly to the partner.
  • Clicking one always shows an interstitial first: it says where you are going and repeats the disclosure before you continue. It is unavoidable — not buried in a footer or behind a hover.
  • The continue link carries rel="sponsored nofollow noopener", marking it as compensated for search engines.
  • Only an allowlist of known partners can be linked, only over HTTPS, and the affiliate tag identifies PriceTruth — it contains nothing about you. Our no-referrer policy means the partner is not even told which page you came from.

Commissions never touch the numbers

True-cost breakdowns, fee estimates, price history, and deal-quality scores are computed before, and completely independently of, any partner relationship. The analysis engine's inputs are the advertised price, the vertical, and our fee datasets; scoring inputs are recorded price history and fee load. Partner status is not an input to either — there is no code path by which being (or not being) a partner changes a number we show. Partners cannot pay for a better score, a lower true price, a hidden fee line, or placement. If a partner's price is a bad deal, the score says so.
